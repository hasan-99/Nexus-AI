Insights

Short notes before building anything.

These are not full blog posts yet. They are the rules I use before recommending AI, SEO, automation, or backend work.

01

Do not automate confusion

If the workflow is unclear, AI only makes the mess faster. Map the process, decision points, and handoffs first.

02

SEO starts with service clarity

Search engines and visitors need the same thing: what you offer, where you serve, who it helps, and what to do next.

03

Small systems win first

The first release should remove one painful step. Once it proves value, expand the system with confidence.
