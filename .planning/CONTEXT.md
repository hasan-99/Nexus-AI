# Hasan Services Website — Domain Glossary

<!-- Domain glossary. Loaded by every road agent. Keep entries terse. -->

## Language

### Service Site
A marketing website that sells Hasan's services to potential clients.
**Avoid:** portfolio when referring to the new direction.

### Visitor
A potential client browsing the website to understand services and decide whether to contact Hasan.
**Avoid:** user, customer until there is an actual product or CRM.

### Lead
A visitor who contacts Hasan or starts a consultation request.
**Avoid:** subscriber, account, user.

### Consultation
The primary conversion action: a visitor asks Hasan to discuss a business problem and possible solution.
**Avoid:** demo unless a real product demo exists.

### Service Offering
A clearly packaged capability Hasan sells, such as AI agents, automation, web design, SEO, or backend systems.
**Avoid:** skill, hobby, stack item.

### Proof
Evidence that builds trust: shipped projects, client outcomes, experience, screenshots, metrics, or testimonials.
**Avoid:** generic claims without evidence.

### Phase
A unit of work inside a milestone. Ends in a verification gate.
**Avoid:** epic, story, ticket, sprint.

### Task
A framework-internal execution unit: one commit-sized work item with one verification contract.
**Avoid:** using task as a client-facing service unit.

## Relationships
- Service Site presents many Service Offerings.
- Service Offering supports one or more business outcomes.
- Visitor reviews Proof before becoming a Lead.
- Consultation is the main conversion path from Visitor to Lead.
- Milestone holds many Phases.
- Phase holds many Tasks.

## Flagged Ambiguities

### "Demo" vs "Consultation"
- **Consultation** = correct CTA for this site today.
- **Demo** = use only if Hasan later offers a productized AI agent demo.

### "Portfolio" vs "Service Site"
- **Portfolio** = old site centered on Hasan.
- **Service Site** = new site centered on client problems and services.
