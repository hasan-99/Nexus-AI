# PRODUCT — Hasan Services Website

> The "who and why" every Qualia agent reads before designing or building anything.

## Register

```
register: brand
secondary: service marketing site
```

Design is the product at this stage. The site must create trust, differentiation, and consultation intent before any app or backend exists.

## Users (specific, not personas)

1. Andreas — owner of a small service business in Nicosia. He opens the site because he wants calls and lead follow-up handled faster without hiring another admin.
2. Maria — manager of a clinic or local office. She wants a modern website, better search visibility, and an easier way for clients to reach the business.
3. Omar — founder of a small software or operations-heavy business. He needs backend/API help, workflow automation, or an AI assistant connected to existing tools.

## Brand Voice

```
Voice: direct, premium, practical
```

Voice in motion: "Tell me the workflow that wastes your time. I will map it, remove the manual steps, and ship the smallest system that proves the business value."

## Anti-References

- Generic personal portfolios with "Hi, I'm..." hero copy — they sell the person before the service and make the visitor work too hard.
- Template agency sites with identical three-card grids — they feel replaceable and do not build technical trust.
- Overloaded corporate consulting pages — too much jargon, too little proof, weak conversion path.

## Positive References

- https://www.qualiasolutions.net/ — premium dark service positioning, strong CTA, AI/Web/SEO service framing, high-confidence technical tone.

## Strategic Principles

- **Services before biography.** The visitor should see what they can buy before learning Hasan's background.
- **Business outcomes before technology.** AI, SEO, and backend systems must be explained through calls answered, leads qualified, admin time saved, and launches shipped.
- **One primary action.** The homepage should drive consultation requests, with WhatsApp and email as supporting routes.
- **Proof beats claims.** Projects, screenshots, metrics, and concrete workflows outrank generic adjectives.
- **Static first.** Keep the implementation lightweight until the site needs real lead storage, CMS, analytics experiments, or auth.

## Constraints

```
Framework:        Static HTML / CSS / JavaScript
Component library: Bootstrap 5 plus custom CSS
Performance:      Fast static load, mobile first, no heavy framework migration in Milestone 1
Accessibility:    WCAG 2.2 AA minimum
Browsers:         Evergreen desktop and mobile browsers
i18n:             English first, Arabic/RTL not in Milestone 1
```

## Differentiation

Visitors remember that Hasan is not just a developer portfolio: the site feels like a focused AI/web automation studio that can remove real operational work from a small business.
