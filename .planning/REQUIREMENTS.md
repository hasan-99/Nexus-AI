# REQUIREMENTS — Hasan Services Website

## Milestone 1 — Service Positioning Foundation

### REQ-001 — Service-Led Homepage
The homepage must communicate services and outcomes before personal biography.

### REQ-002 — Clear Service Offerings
The site must present AI agents, business automation, web design, SEO, and backend/API systems as buyable service offerings.

### REQ-003 — Conversion CTA
The site must include a clear primary consultation CTA and supporting WhatsApp/email/LinkedIn contact routes.

### REQ-004 — Proof Reframing
Existing projects and experience must be reframed as trust-building proof for services.

### REQ-005 — Service SEO
Metadata, headings, and structured data must reflect service/business intent, not only personal profile intent.

### REQ-006 — Responsive Verification
The final page must be browser-checked at mobile, tablet, and desktop sizes.

## Milestone 2 — Proof + Conversion Depth

### REQ-007 — Case Study Depth
Add richer case-study/project blocks that describe outcome, service delivered, and technologies used.

### REQ-008 — Service Detail Expansion
Add enough detail for each service that a visitor can understand fit, process, and next step.

### REQ-009 — Lead Flow Decision
Decide whether contact remains mail/WhatsApp or needs a real form/backend.

## Milestone 3 — Handoff

### REQ-010 — Production Readiness
Ship final polish, SEO files, link checks, deployment verification, and maintenance notes.
