# Research Summary

## Stack Recommendation
Stay static for Milestone 1. The fastest path to value is repositioning the existing site into a service-led homepage, not migrating frameworks.

## Table Stakes
1. Service-led hero and navigation.
2. Clear services: AI agents, automation, web design, SEO, backend/API systems.
3. Consultation CTA and visible contact routes.
4. Proof framed as outcomes.
5. SEO metadata aligned to services.
6. Browser-verified responsive layout.

## Top Pitfall
The site currently wants to become two things at once: a loud personal portfolio and a premium services business. The next build must choose services first and move personal biography below proof.

## Suggested Milestone Arc
1. **Service Positioning Foundation** — rewrite structure, hero, services, CTA, proof, and SEO on the static homepage.
2. **Proof + Conversion Depth** — improve case studies, contact flow, analytics readiness, and dedicated service detail.
3. **Launch + Handoff** — final polish, SEO, QA, deployment documentation, and owner handoff.
