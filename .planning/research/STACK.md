# Research — Stack

## Recommendation
Keep the first milestone on the existing static stack: `index.html`, `css/style.css`, `script/script.js`, Bootstrap 5, Font Awesome, and static hosting.

## Why
- The business problem is positioning and conversion, not application functionality.
- The existing site already runs locally with `python -m http.server`.
- Static HTML gives fast iteration for copy, layout, SEO, and visual direction.

## Upgrade Trigger
Move to Next.js/Vercel only when one of these becomes real:
- CMS-managed case studies or blog publishing.
- Real lead capture with validation, persistence, email notifications, or CRM sync.
- A/B testing, analytics experiments, or service landing page variants.
- Multilingual routing or structured content at scale.

## Milestone Implication
Milestone 1 should not migrate frameworks. Milestone 2 can add richer service pages and content. Milestone 3 can decide whether static remains enough.
