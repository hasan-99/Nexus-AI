# Research — Features

## Table Stakes for Milestone 1
- Service-led hero with clear business promise.
- Primary CTA: book consultation / talk to Hasan.
- Service offering section: AI agents, automation, web design, SEO, backend/API systems.
- Proof section: projects, experience, screenshots, outcomes, Cyprus/local credibility.
- Contact section with WhatsApp, email, LinkedIn, and simple inquiry path.
- SEO metadata updated for services, not only a personal profile.
- Responsive QA across mobile, tablet, desktop.

## Strong Differentiators
- Workflow-first service cards: "calls answered", "leads qualified", "admin removed", "site launched".
- Mini diagnostic CTA: "Tell me the workflow wasting your time."
- Case-study style project cards that explain result, not just show a screenshot.
- "Service menu" that feels like a studio offer rather than a CV skill list.

## Later Features
- Dedicated service pages.
- Lead form backed by email or database.
- Analytics and conversion event tracking.
- Case-study CMS.
- Blog/content SEO system.
- Arabic or bilingual service page.
