# ROADMAP — Milestone 1: Service Positioning Foundation

## Goal
Convert the current personal portfolio homepage into a service-led business website that sells AI, web design, SEO, automation, and backend engineering services.

## Phase 1 — Service IA + Copy

### Outcome
The page structure and copy are service-first.

### Tasks
- Replace "about me" navigation language with service-led labels.
- Rewrite hero copy around business outcomes and consultation.
- Define service offerings and outcome bullets.
- Move personal/about content below services and proof.

### Verification
- First viewport answers: what is offered, who it helps, what action to take.
- No "Hi, I'm..." hero as primary message.

## Phase 2 — Homepage Visual System

### Outcome
The visual direction feels like a premium AI/web service studio.

### Tasks
- Replace the temporary loud portfolio treatment with a focused dark service-studio hero.
- Use the portrait as secondary trust, not the main product.
- Style service cards, CTA, and proof sections consistently.
- Remove or neutralize obsolete personal-portfolio visual clutter.

### Verification
- Browser screenshot at 1280x720 shows full hero and CTA without clipping.
- Mobile screenshot shows hero message and CTA clearly.

## Phase 3 — Proof, Contact + SEO

### Outcome
The site supports trust and conversion.

### Tasks
- Reframe project cards as service proof.
- Update contact section around consultation.
- Update title, description, Open Graph, and structured data for service intent.
- Ensure WhatsApp, email, LinkedIn, and CV links still work.

### Verification
- SEO metadata no longer describes only a personal profile.
- All contact links resolve.

## Phase 4 — Responsive QA

### Outcome
The service site is ready for launch review.

### Tasks
- Run browser QA at 375, 768, 1280, and 1440 widths.
- Check console errors.
- Check visual overlap, clipped text, and broken nav anchors.
- Produce final notes for remaining Milestone 2 work.

### Verification
- No horizontal scroll.
- No hero or CTA clipping.
- No critical console errors.
- Local URL renders correctly.
