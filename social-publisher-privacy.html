Hasan Social Publisher
Privacy notice

Your data stays on your computer where possible.

Effective date: 17 October 2026

Who operates this tool. Hasan Social Publisher is a private local application operated by Hasan Jahoush in Nicosia, Cyprus. It runs on the operator’s Windows computer and is not a public account service. Privacy questions or requests can be sent to hasan.cy99@gmail.com.

Information stored locally

The app stores the operator’s content profile and settings, topic ideas, generated or edited drafts, hashtags, visual briefs, locally rendered images, schedule, publishing history, and limited technical audit and AI usage records. These are kept in the project’s local SQLite database and data files. The local dashboard uses a session cookie to keep the operator signed in.

OAuth credentials for Google, LinkedIn, and ChatGPT are stored on the same computer in encrypted local files. Google and LinkedIn profile identifiers and account email/name details are stored only as needed to show which account is connected. The app does not store the operator’s account passwords.

Google data and Gmail permission

The app requests Google identity information to show the connected account and the Gmail API permission https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send. It uses that permission only to send the operator publishing-success notifications and failure alerts at hasan.cy99@gmail.com. A success notice may include the published text, LinkedIn post link, and the locally rendered image attachment. The app does not read, search, or modify Gmail messages, contacts, or inbox settings. Google user data is not sold or used for advertising.

Content generation with ChatGPT

If ChatGPT plan usage is connected and enabled, the app sends the configured writing profile, the selected topic, recent post excerpts used to avoid repetition, and any selected public-source excerpts to OpenAI for post generation. This request uses the separately authorized usage allowance for the operator’s eligible ChatGPT plan. The app does not use an OpenAI API key or paid API fallback. The app requests non-stored Responses API output; OpenAI handles requests under its applicable terms and data controls. The app does not access ChatGPT conversations or memory.

LinkedIn and publishing

The app uses LinkedIn account identity and its authorized posting permission to publish configured content to the operator’s personal profile. Posts published to LinkedIn are public or visible according to the operator’s LinkedIn settings and LinkedIn’s policies. LinkedIn processes account and post data under its own privacy notice.

Sharing and service providers

The app sends information only to the services needed for features the operator enabled: OpenAI for eligible text generation, Google for account authorization and requested email delivery, and LinkedIn for account authorization and publishing. It also retrieves selected public updates from official OpenAI, GitHub, and Microsoft feeds when current sources are requested. n8n communicates with the dashboard over the same computer’s loopback network. The public information site is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may process ordinary web request data under GitHub’s terms.

Retention and deletion

App content and history remain on the operator’s computer until the operator deletes the local project data. Disconnecting an integration removes that integration’s local credentials, but does not erase post history. To remove all locally stored app data, stop the local services and delete the project’s data folder. Any information already sent to OpenAI, Google, or LinkedIn is handled under those providers’ policies and account controls.

Security and changes

The dashboard and n8n service bind to localhost. OAuth refresh tokens are encrypted at rest using a key derived from the local app secret. The computer’s account, disk, and backups remain the operator’s responsibility. This notice will be updated if the app’s data practices change.

Contact

For access, deletion, or privacy questions about this local tool, email hasan.cy99@gmail.com.
