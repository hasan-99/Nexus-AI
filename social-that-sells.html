Social media that builds a business, not just a feed

By Hasan Jahoush·Jan 2026·5 min read

Followers are a vanity number. Customers are a business. The two are related, but they are not the same thing, and confusing them is how companies waste years posting beautifully into the void.

The question is never "how do we get more followers?" It is "how do we turn attention into trust, and trust into customers?" Everything else is decoration.

Pick the platform your customers actually use

You do not have to be everywhere. Be genuinely excellent on the one or two channels where your customers already spend their time, and ignore the rest without an ounce of guilt. A focused presence on the right platform beats a thin, exhausted presence spread across five.

Every post should have a job

If a post is not doing one of these jobs, it is just noise:

  • Proof — a result, a before-and-after, a happy customer in their own words.
  • Teaching — one tip the reader can actually use today.
  • Action — an offer, an event, a clear and easy next step.
  • Presence — a simple reminder that you exist and you are good at this.

Consistency beats brilliance

The account that posts something decent every week will beat the one that posts something brilliant twice a year. Show up, be useful, stay recognisable, and let the compounding do the slow, quiet work.

Social done well is not a popularity contest. It is a steady stream of small reasons for the right people to trust you, until buying from you feels like the obvious choice.
