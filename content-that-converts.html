Content that sounds like you, and sells

By Hasan Jahoush·Feb 2026·4 min read

The mistake most businesses make with content is writing for everyone, which in practice means writing for no one. The fix is almost embarrassingly simple: picture one real customer and talk to them like a human.

Write to one person

When the words sound like a person who understands the reader's problem — not a brand broadcasting its features — people stay, trust, and act. Helpfully specific beats broadly impressive every single time. Name the exact worry your customer has at eleven at night, and answer it honestly. That is the whole trick.

Useful is the strategy

Do that consistently and something quietly powerful happens: the same posts that win customers tend to please the algorithm too, because both reward content that genuinely answers a question. You are not gaming a system; you are simply being the most useful result, which is the only durable way to earn attention online.

A rhythm you can keep

Content does not reward heroics. It rewards showing up:

  • One clear idea per piece — resist the urge to say everything at once.
  • A voice that matches how you actually speak to clients.
  • A pace you can sustain for a year, not a frantic month followed by silence.

Content is not about going viral. It is about being the business that had already answered the question by the time the customer thought to ask it.
